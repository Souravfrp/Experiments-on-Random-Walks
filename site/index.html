Interpretation: Each point is drawn independently and uniformly from the bounding cube [−1−λ,1+λ]n. A point belongs to P+λB when its Euclidean distance to P is at most λ; its component index counts coordinates outside [−1,1]. The reported intervals are approximate Wilson binomial intervals rescaled to volumes/coefficient estimates, valid as a sampling uncertainty guide for this independent-sample model only. Rare components and high dimensions can make this simple rejection scheme inefficient. It is not B1/B2/B3, a proof of a polynomial-time algorithm, or a controlled Python/C++ benchmark.

PROVENANCE

What belongs in one comparison?

Changing hardware, compiler flags, the number of C++ threads, or how random draws are generated changes what a timing comparison means. The archive therefore labels Mac transcriptions separately from development-environment examples. A single seed is not evidence of a confidence interval.

MATHEMATICAL CONTROL

Coordinate Hit-and-Run

From the origin, the exact total-variation distance equals the probability that at least one coordinate has not yet been updated. For n = 20, its 0.1-TV mixing time is 103 steps. The recorded empirical proxy of 64 steps is not a substitute for that result.
